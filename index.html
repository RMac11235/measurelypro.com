Voice estimating for flooring and lighting contractors. For Android and iPhone.

Speak the job. Approve the numbers. Save the estimate.

Measurely is built for flooring and lighting contractors who want to measure by voice, then review what was captured before an estimate or invoice is saved.

Measure by voice

Record the job with the microphone. The transcript is saved on the device first, so you can capture the work even when you are offline. It syncs when you are back online.

Review before you save

Approve the numbers before an estimate or invoice can be saved. Uncertain wording is flagged instead of guessed.

Flooring

The app handles area, waste, stairs, and carton rounding from the measurements you approve.

Lighting

Price counts from a built-in book covering recessed lights, fixture swaps, under-cabinet lighting, switches, dimmers, circuits, and stairs.

English and Spanish

The record and sign-in screens switch between English and Spanish. Speech recognition follows the language you choose.

Share a measurement sheet

Build a PDF measurement sheet on the phone and share it from the device.

How it works

  1. 1

    Record the job

    Speak the measurements. The transcript is stored on the phone first and can be captured offline, then sent in when you have a connection.

  2. 2

    Review and approve

    Check the numbers. Anything uncertain is flagged for you, and nothing becomes an estimate or invoice until you approve it.

  3. 3

    Estimate, invoice, and share

    Save the estimate or invoice on the spot, and share a PDF measurement sheet when you need a record of the job.
